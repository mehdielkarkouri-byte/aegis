Method & limits

PIPELINE
  • Ingest → team goals for/against from the UEFA league-phase tables.
  • Rate estimation: attack and defence per match, divided by the competition mean, then shrunk toward 1 with weight n/(n+4).
  • λhome = μ · atth · defa · 1.08, λaway = μ · atta · defh.
  • Three score distributions (DC, BP, NB) → weighted blend.
  • 200,000 Monte Carlo draws with ±15% parameter noise → stability of the leading score.
  • Verdict tier from probability and stability, never from model agreement alone.
WHAT THIS CANNOT DO
  • Produce a certain exact score. The best candidate in this board is ≈13%.
  • Use xG, lineups, injuries, referees or weather: no feed is connected.
  • Claim calibration: no walk-forward archive exists yet for 2026/27.
  • Treat a 2–4 match sample as a stable team strength.
TRACEABILITY

Each fixture stores its λ, the five model outputs, the Monte Carlo leader and the stability rate, so any verdict can be audited after the match.
